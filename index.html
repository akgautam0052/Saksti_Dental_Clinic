About Sakthi Dental Clinic

Get to Know Dr. Anupriya - Your Trusted Partner in Dental Care

Bringing over 20 years of expertise, Dr. Anupriya is a leading figure in modern dentistry at Hosur. She began her career after graduating in 2000 and spent six impactful years at Mathura Clinic, refining her clinical skills. In 2004, she established Sakthi Dental Clinic in Hosur to make high-quality dental care accessible to all.

She has also served as a dental consultant at the Primary Health Center at Chandara Hospital for over a decade. Here, advanced dental technology meets a warm, patient-friendly environment. Because here, your smile isn't just treated, it's celebrated.

Information We Collect

Sakthi Dental Clinic collects personal details—specifically patient name, contact mobile number, email address, appointment date/time preferences, and optional chief dental concerns—strictly when voluntarily submitted via our appointment booking or contact forms. We do not employ automated surveillance or background profiling.

Cookies

Our website uses basic browser local storage (localStorage) exclusively to preserve your preferred color scheme (light or dark mode) and to store client-side appointment references locally on your device. We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, trackers, or commercial pixels.

How We Use Your Information

The submitted contact details are utilized solely for scheduling, managing, and confirming your clinical appointments, providing clinical triage, sending appointment reminders, and responding to your enquiries.

Data Security

We prioritize patient privacy with industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards. Our client forms run without third-party data broker integrations to guard against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

Information Sharing

We maintain a strict non-disclosure standard: Sakthi Dental Clinic will never sell, lease, trade, or monetize your personal health information, phone numbers, or email addresses with any commercial entities or third-party marketers.

Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Protection)

While we proudly provide pediatric dentistry to young patients, our online scheduling interface is intended exclusively for use by parents, legal guardians, and adult family members over the age of 18. We do not knowingly solicit personal data directly from children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may revise this privacy statement periodically to reflect changes in regulatory standards or clinic policies. Any updates will be published immediately on this page with an updated revision date.

Acceptance of Terms

By accessing our website and scheduling your consultation with Sakthi Dental Clinic, you acknowledge and agree to the guidelines set forth in this privacy policy.
